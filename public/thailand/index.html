About this notebook & credits

Trip photographs are a curated selection from the traveller’s Thailand collection. Dates and times follow the album’s local capture times in Thailand (UTC+7). Named stops use visible signs, distinctive architecture, photographed brochures, location data and adjacent sequences; uncertain meal matches are marked as inferred. General place history is sourced separately from personal observations. Suggested but unrecorded stops are not included. Inline galleries show the photographs in place; tap any image for the full-size viewer. Online reference photographs are labelled separately.
